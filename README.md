## About Me

I'm a **Fullstack Software Engineer** holding a Bachelor of Science in Informatics from Universitas Atma Jaya Yogyakarta (GPA 3.76), focused on building reliable software across enterprise systems, modern web applications, and robust backend architectures.

Currently, I have hands-on experience building enterprise tools, optimization systems, and fullstack applications ranging from corporate asset management systems to real-time scheduling platforms and e-commerce solutions.

My strongest areas are:
- Enterprise backend development, data extraction, validation pipelines, and technology lifecycle management programs
- Fullstack product development with Laravel, React, Next.js, Node.js, Java, and SQL databases
- Designing interactive booking systems, database architecture, and real-time state management (Socket.io)
- Translating complex business and operational requirements into functional, clean production code

## Featured Work

### FLORALESS — Real-Time Booking & Scheduling Platform
Fullstack web-based client booking and scheduling application integrated with a Socket.io-driven Real-Time Availability Calendar.
**Stack:** React, Node.js, Socket.io, PostgreSQL, Express, Railway

### Enterprise Tooling & Technology Lifecycle Systems
Developed internal analytical tools and systems used for calculating Cost Per Transaction (CPT) metrics, extracting, validating, and structuring IT asset and utilization data, alongside building custom string-matching programs to streamline technology lifecycle management (EoS/EoL).
**Stack:** Java, Python, SQL, Internal Corporate Infrastructure

### BaratieResto — Restaurant Management & Reservation
Comprehensive restaurant management and online table reservation platform designed for end-to-end ordering and operational workflows.
**Stack:** PHP, Laravel, MySQL, JavaScript, Bootstrap/Tailwind

### Pandanara & Reusemart — Digital Commerce Platforms
Web-based catalog, ordering platform, and secondhand goods commerce ecosystems built to support digital infrastructure and user-item management for businesses.
**Stack:** PHP, Laravel, JavaScript, MySQL, Tailwind CSS
